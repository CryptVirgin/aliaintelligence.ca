Privacy Policy

ALIA Intelligence Inc. · Last updated July 2026

ALIA Intelligence Inc. ("ALIA", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains the limited information we collect through this website and how we handle it, consistent with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

Contact form. If you use our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide — solely so we can respond to your inquiry. Submissions are delivered to us by email via our form provider (Formspree).

Website analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first tool that measures aggregate traffic (such as page views and general region) without cookies and without tracking or identifying individual visitors.

How we use it

We use the information you send only to respond to you and to understand overall site traffic. We do not use it for advertising or automated profiling.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties except as needed to operate this site (for example, our form and hosting providers) or where required by law.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have sent us. Email info@aliaintelligence.ca and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at info@aliaintelligence.ca.
