( How it is made )

Five steps from an idea to every screen.

The same pipeline runs every show. Each step has a check before the next one starts.

  1. Step 1

    Script

    A human idea and direction, a full end to end script, read and approved before any picture exists.

  2. Step 2

    Pictures

    Native 4K pictures, drawn to match the words, with layout guides so a building has the right number of windows.

  3. Step 3

    Voice

    One natural take per scene, checked word by word against the script, never stitched line by line.

  4. Step 4

    Video

    Slow movement and long dissolves, built in 4K, then checked against each platform's rules before it ships.

  5. Step 5

    Publish

    Reviewed privately, then released on every platform together, with the AI line on all of them.

Every picture on this page comes from our own shows. No stock photos, no borrowed characters.
